Privacy Policy

How Fuego handles your data.

Last updated: 25 October 2025

Introduction

At Fuego, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when using the bot. As a third-party application, Fuego also adheres to the Discord Terms of Service and Discord Privacy Policy.

Data Collection

Fuego collects only the information necessary to perform its functions. We do not collect personal information such as names, addresses, or emails. The data we collect includes:

  • Music play history
  • Server settings (such as the 247 and prefix)
  • Command usage and interaction logs

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely for improving the functionality of the bot and providing the best user experience. We do not share or sell your data with third parties for any reason.

Data Retention

Fuego does not retain user data for long periods. Your interaction data is stored temporarily and deleted after the session ends or after a reasonable period of inactivity.

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the bot will indicate your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to contact us through our official support channels.
